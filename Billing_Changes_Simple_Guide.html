New billing page — simple guide

What changed on /billing (Spec v2.0). Written for a quick read.

1. One place tells the truth

Before, the page could show different plan names in different places.

Now everything uses one state:

  • Above the slider = what you have now (current plan)
  • Inside the slider = what you are looking at (selected plan)

Header cards show: Current plan · Credits left (e.g. 6 of 30) · Renews / Ends

2. The four plans (signed-off prices)

Plan Inspections / month £ per inspection
Starter 10 – 29 £4.90
Growth 30 – 74 £4.30
Professional 75 – 199 £3.70
Enterprise 200+ By quote only

Move the slider and you always see the £ per inspection under “Selected plan”.

Enterprise boundary — locked

199 = Professional (last self-serve priced volume)
200 = Enterprise (quote-only)
Slider max = 200 — you can land on 200; CTA becomes Request a quote
Do not cap the slider at 199.

At 200+, prices hide and the button becomes Request a quote.

3. Annual is the default

New customers see Annual selected first (20% off).

Example Growth, 30 inspections:

Monthly = 30 × £4.30 = £129 / month
Annual billed = £129 × 12 × 0.80 = £1,238.40 / year
You save £309.60 vs paying monthly all year
Note: £1,548 (= £129 × 12 with no discount) is list-only — never show it as the annual bill. Note: £1,548 (= £129 × 12 with no discount) is list-only — never show it as the annual bill.

If you already pay monthly, the page keeps Monthly selected for you.

4. Top-up packs (extra credits)

Bigger packs cost less per inspection. Growth example:

Pack Total Per inspection
20 £95 £4.75
50 £225 £4.50
100 £430 £4.30 (lowest rate)

The 100-pack badge is Lowest rate (not “Best value”). Top-ups are priced from your current plan, not the slider.

5. What counts as “one inspection”

One credit = up to 300 photos.

  • 1 – 300 photos → 1 credit
  • 301 – 600 photos → 2 credits
  • 601 – 900 photos → 3 credits

The header says Credits left.

On mobile, if you take ~280+ photos, you get a warning before you finish: this job will use more than one credit.

6. Extra modules (locked by plan)

Six add-ons. Moving the slider unlocks them:

  • Starter — all locked
  • Growth+ — Tenant Portal, Dispute Portal, Extra retention
  • Professional+ — also IVY bots + White labelling

Tenant Portal price depends on how many properties/units you manage, not the slider number.

7. Helpful bits for buyers

  • Trial: no plan yet? “Run 3 inspections free” (no card)
  • Almost out of credits (80%+): banner suggests the next plan and moves the slider for you
  • FAQ: 8 questions, including the 300-photo rule and 24-month photo storage

8. Plan change money (proration) — locked

Every Stripe plan/tier update uses proration_behavior = "always_invoice" (shared/stripeProrationPolicy.ts

Upgrade today → pay the prorated difference now (Stripe invoice).
Downgrade today → no cash refund; unused time becomes account credit on the next invoice(s).
Next renewal → full new plan price (credits applied automatically).
Never use none or bare create_prorations for plan changes.

9. Renewal timezone — locked UTC

All renewal / expiry decisions use UTC (shared/billingClock.ts).

Not customer TZ. Not server OS TZ.
Source of truth: Stripe current_period_end (Unix → UTC).
UI shows dates like 31 Jul 2026 UTC.

10. Currencies — locked list

Supported: GBP (base), USD, EUR, AED.

Not supported: ZAR (and any other code).

Catalogue rates are authored in GBP, then converted.
Currency is locked while a subscription is active.
Source: shared/billingCurrencies.ts
Remember: Enterprise never shows a public £ rate on the page. From 200 inspections/month upward, it’s quote only.
