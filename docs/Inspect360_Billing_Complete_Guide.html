1. Big picture

Billing has two layers that work together:

  • Subscription (Stripe) — what plan you pay for, how often (monthly/annual), renewal date, and modules.
  • Credit wallet (batches) — the actual inspection balance. Credits come from several sources and may expire on different dates.

The Billing page shows wallet truth: Credits Left = all usable batches (plan + signup/bonus + packs), not plan-only.

Rule of thumb Paying for a plan grants a monthly/annual quota of plan credits. Buying packs or receiving bonuses adds extra batches. Completing an inspection spends from the wallet in a fixed priority order.

2. Tiers & pricing

Self-serve tiers are chosen by how many inspections/month you need on the slider (10–200).

Plan price formulas

  • Monthly: n × tierRate
  • Annual (billed): n × tierRate × 12 × 0.80 — always apply the 20% annual discount
Locked rule — Growth 30 example Monthly = 30 × £4.30 = £129 / month
Annual billed = £129 × 12 × 0.80 = £1,238.40 / year
You save £309.60 vs paying monthly all year (£1,548).
Do not use the undiscounted “Annual value” Some older spec tables showed “Annual £1,548” (= £129 × 12 with no 0.80). That figure is only the list cost of 12 months at the monthly rate. It is not what we charge or show as “Billed annually”. Source of truth: annual = monthly × 12 × 0.80.

Slider behaviour

  • Min 10, max 200 (= quote gate). Not capped at 199.
  • Near tier boundaries (within 3 of a snap point: 10 / 30 / 75 / 200) the slider snaps to that point.
  • 1–199: self-serve price + Subscribe / Upgrade / Change plan.
  • ≥200: CTA becomes Request a quote — no self-serve published Enterprise rate.

3. What a “credit” is (BILL-08)

One inspection credit covers up to 300 photos. Larger inspections consume more credits:

PhotosCredits consumed
1 – 3001
301 – 6002
601 – 9003
…MAX(1, CEIL(photos / 300))
Edge: zero / empty photo count Completing still requires at least 1 credit even if photo count is 0 (creditsConsumed(0) → 1).

UI warns around 280 photos that you are approaching the next credit.

Some features cost fixed credits (e.g. comparison reports may require 2).

4. Credit wallet & sources

Every grant creates a batch with: quantity, remaining, source, granted date, optional expiry.

Source How you get it Expires? On plan change On period renewal
plan_inclusion Included with subscription quota Yes — usually period end / renewal date Converted to bonus (keeps same expiry) Expired / wiped, then new quota granted
admin_grant (bonus) Signup welcome (5), admin grants, leftovers converted from plan Signup: never. Converted leftovers: original plan expiry Untouched Only if expiresAt has passed; signup bonus survives renewal
addon_pack Purchased credit packs Yes — aligned to subscription renewal date Untouched (still packs) Expired with plan reset (no pack rollover)
topup Legacy / one-time top-up orders Depends on grant Untouched Expires if date passed
refund / adjustment Corrections / ledger adjustments Depends Untouched Expires if date passed
Key product decision (Jul 2026) Mid-cycle upgrade/downgrade does not wipe leftover plan credits. They are reclassified as bonus and still reset/expire when their original expiry date arrives.

5. Consume order

When an inspection (or other billed action) spends N credits, batches are used in this order:

1
Bonus first — admin_grant, refund, adjustment (including converted leftovers + signup)
2
Packs / top-ups — addon_pack, topup
3
Plan last — plan_inclusion

Within the same group: earliest expiry first, then oldest grant (FIFO).

Why this order? Time-limited or free/bonus credits burn before paid plan quota, so customers don’t lose expiring value while plan credits sit unused.
Insufficient credits If the wallet total < credits needed, the action is blocked (create/complete inspection, some AI/report features). Having an active subscription with 0 remaining still blocks until you renew, buy a pack, or receive a grant.

6. Signup & free trial

ConceptValueNotes
Signup reward 5 credits Granted once as admin_grant. No expiry. Idempotent (won’t double-grant).
Free trial messaging 3 inspections Marketing/UI bridge: “run up to 3 free” while you still have signup credits and no plan.
No plan required — You can spend signup credits with status: none. CTA is Subscribe & Pay.
Edge: trial vs wallet Trial “used” is inferred from signup credits spent (≥3 of the 5), or once any plan exists. Remaining signup credits after “trial” still sit in the wallet until spent — they do not auto-expire.

8. Upgrade & downgrade (plan change)

Applies to tier-based change, legacy change-plan, and Stripe subscription updates that change quota.

1
Stripe / DB subscription updated to new tier + quota + period.
2
Convert remaining plan_inclusion batches → admin_grant (bonus). Quantity & expiresAt unchanged.
3
Grant full new plan quota as fresh plan_inclusion.
Leftovers → bonus Example: 4 plan credits left, expire in 12 days. After upgrade those 4 become bonus, still expire in 12 days. You also get the full new monthly allocation immediately.

What does not convert

  • Existing signup bonus / other admin grants
  • Purchased addon packs
  • Already-expired batches

CTA labels

  • Higher tier → Upgrade plan
  • Lower tier or period change → Change plan
  • Same tier + same period → disabled: “This is your current plan”
  • past_due → payment method path
Edge: double balance after change Wallet can temporarily show converted leftovers + full new quota. That is intentional. Leftovers still die on their original expiry; they are not permanent extras.

8b. Money proration (Stripe) — locked policy

Plan-change money is calculated by Stripe, not by hand-rolled day math in our app. Every subscriptions.update for a plan/tier change must use the same behaviour.

Locked constant proration_behavior = "always_invoice"
Defined in shared/stripeProrationPolicy.ts → PLAN_CHANGE_PRORATION_BEHAVIOR. Use planChangeStripeUpdateParams() — do not hard-code other values on plan changes.

Why always_invoice (not the other options)

ValueWhat it doesInspect360?
create_prorations Creates proration line items; they often wait until the next invoice Not for plan changes — money timing ambiguous
always_invoice Creates prorations and finalises an invoice immediately Required for all plan/tier changes
none No money adjustment for the rest of the period Forbidden for plan changes

How Stripe calculates (we do not re-implement)

1
Credit — unused time remaining on the old price
2
Charge — new price for the remaining days in the period
3
Invoice now — net = charge − credit (via always_invoice)

Answers developers need

QuestionAnswer
Upgrade today → customer pays what? The net prorated difference today (immediate Stripe invoice). Unused old-plan time is credited against the new plan for the rest of the period.
Downgrade today → refund? No cash refund. Unused old-plan time becomes a Stripe customer credit / balance.
Downgrade today → credit? Yes — account credit applied automatically on future invoices.
What about the next invoice? At renewal, customer is billed the full new plan price; any remaining credit balance is applied by Stripe.
Does period end move? Same interval (monthly→monthly / annual→annual): period end stays. Changing monthly ↔ annual follows Stripe interval-change rules.
Inspection credits vs money? Separate. Money = Stripe proration. Credits = convert leftovers to bonus + grant new quota (see §8).
Example — upgrade mid-month
Was: Growth £129/mo. Day 10 of 30-day cycle → ~20 days left.
Upgrade to Professional £277.50/mo equivalent for remaining days.
Stripe credits unused Growth time, charges Professional for remaining days,
invoices the net difference today.
Exact pence come from Stripe’s clock — trust the invoice line items.
Example — downgrade mid-month
Was: Professional. Downgrade to Growth with days left.
Stripe credits unused Professional time, charges Growth for remaining days.
Net is usually a credit — no bank refund; applies to next invoice(s).
Do not invent parallel formulas Module mid-cycle purchases may still use proRataService for one-off Checkout line items. Plan/tier subscription updates must not mix in custom day-count money math — Stripe + always_invoice only.

9. Renewal & monthly reset

Triggered by Stripe invoice.paid (tier renewal) and/or the scheduled monthly reset job when renewal date ≤ now (UTC — see §9b).

What happens on renewal

  1. processCreditExpiry — expire any batch where expiresAt <= now (UTC timestamps only; includes converted bonus leftovers that hit their date).
  2. Force-expire remaining plan_inclusion (no plan rollover into the next cycle).
  3. Force-expire remaining addon_pack (packs do not roll into the next cycle).
  4. Grant new plan_inclusion = current quota, with new period expiry.
  5. Advance subscriptionRenewalDate by 1 month or 1 year.
Critical distinction
  • Mid-cycle plan change → leftovers convert to bonus (keep expiry).
  • Period renewal → leftover plan and packs are reset (no rollover). Converted bonus only drops if its expiry date has arrived.
Signup bonus on renewal Signup’s 5 credits have expiresAt = null, so renewal does not wipe them. They remain until spent.

9b. Renewal timezone — locked to UTC

Billing must never leave timezone undefined. Inspect360 uses UTC only for renewal, period-end, and credit-expiry decisions.

Locked constant BILLING_TIMEZONE = "UTC" in shared/billingClock.ts.
QuestionAnswer
Timezone? UTC — always.
Customer timezone? Not used for charging, renewal eligibility, or credit expiry.
Server OS timezone? Not used — do not rely on host local time.
Source of truth for period end? Stripe current_period_end (Unix seconds → UTC). Mirrored to subscription_renewal_date.
When is renewal “due”? subscription_renewal_date <= billingNowUtc() (instant compare).
Credit expiry rule? expiresAt <= now — UTC timestamps only (both sides absolute instants via isAtOrPastBillingInstant / billingNowUtc).
UI display? Show UTC calendar date via formatBillingDateUtcLocale() (e.g. 31 Jul 2026 UTC). Display conversion must still force timeZone: "UTC".
Credit expiry comparison Rule: expiresAt <= now. Both values are UTC timestamps only. Never interpret expiresAt as a local calendar date — that causes off-by-one timezone bugs.
Forbidden
  • toLocaleDateString() without timeZone: "UTC" for billing dates
  • setMonth / setFullYear in local time when advancing renewals — use nextRenewalUtc()
  • Per-org or per-user “office hours” timezone for period boundaries

Available for self-serve tiers (not Enterprise quote-only). Pricing (v2):

  • Top-up rate = tierRate × 1.10
  • Pack sizes: 20 / 50 / 100 with discounts 0% / 5% / 9%
  • packTotal = round(topUpRate × discount × size) (nearest £1)
Pack sizeDiscount multiplier

  • Granted as addon_pack.
  • Expiry set to current subscription renewal date.
  • Consumed after bonus, before plan.
  • Unused pack credits are cleared on period reset (no rollover).
  • Purchase flow is idempotent (won’t double-grant the same Stripe session).

13. When actions are blocked

  • Create / complete inspection — blocked if wallet < credits needed for photo count.
  • Comparison reports / some AI — fixed credit costs; 402 if insufficient.
  • Enterprise self-serve — must request quote (≥200).
  • Currency change — blocked while an active subscription exists.
  • Packs on Enterprise / no plan — self-serve packs are for active self-serve tiers.

→ 4 plan become bonus still expiring 12 Apr.

16. Quick FAQ

Do unused plan credits roll into next month?

No. On renewal they reset. Mid-cycle leftovers only survive by converting to bonus — and that bonus still expires on the old date.

Do packs roll over?

No. Packs expire with the subscription period / are cleared on monthly reset.

Does the signup bonus expire?

No — unless you spend it. It has no expiresAt.

After upgrade, why did my balance go up a lot?

You keep leftovers (as bonus) and receive the full new quota. Leftovers are temporary until their expiry.

Upgrade today — what do I pay?

The prorated net difference today (Stripe invoice via always_invoice). Unused old-plan time is credited against the new plan for the rest of the period.

Downgrade today — refund or credit?

Credit only — no cash refund. Unused time becomes Stripe customer balance and reduces future invoices. Next renewal is the full new (lower) plan price.

What timezone is renewal?

UTC only. Not customer timezone, not server OS timezone. Stripe current_period_end (Unix) is mirrored to subscription_renewal_date. UI shows dates with a UTC label via formatBillingDateUtcLocale().

Which currencies are supported?

GBP, USD, EUR, AED. Base catalogue is GBP. ZAR is not supported. Currency is locked while a subscription is active. See shared/billingCurrencies.ts.

Which credits get used first?

Bonus → packs/top-ups → plan. Within each group, soonest-to-expire first.

What is Enterprise?

199 = Professional (self-serve). 200+ = Enterprise (quote-only). The slider maximum is 200 so users can land on the quote gate; at 200 the CTA is Request a quote, not Subscribe. No published Enterprise rate or standard pack UI.
